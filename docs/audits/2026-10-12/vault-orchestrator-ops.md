## Checked and sound

- **Lock order:**
  - CLI and child units take HEAVY then INDEX (`ops.py:71`, `wiring.py:452,467`).
  - The vault takes source, then generation, then record (`locks.py`). Same-thread re-entry is tracked, and an upgrade raises.
  - The ledger `flock` is innermost: it is taken only in `_append` and never wraps a vault lock.
  - Rollback promotes **outside** the generation lock (`repair.py:191-198`).
  - No cycle was found. A hung NAS write can still hold the ledger lock without a timeout (convoy, not deadlock).
- **Busy means defer** works in production: the journal shows `kind: busy`, then retry after 10 min, with no attempts counted.
- **Store:** `row_version` optimistic updates, and `BEGIN IMMEDIATE` with COMMIT/ROLLBACK (`store.py:286-310, 402-442`).
- **Crash accounting** (`pipeline.py:410-430, 1108-1150`) and unit `StartLimit*`. A WSL shutdown during a heavy step counts as a crash, by design.
- **Seal is resumable after a crash:** the staged manifest is re-validated against the record (`vault.py:654-730`).
- **Adopt** recovers after a crash at the rename step and at the publish (rename path).
- **Scrub** separates MISSING from UNREADABLE (`scrub.py:537-553`); a missing manifest or signature is a SignatureError, not an I/O error.
- **Ledger:** truncate-on-failure plus dirty-hold, and `recover` under the writer lock.
- **API exposure:**
  - loopback bind (`--allow-remote` required otherwise);
  - Host allow-list;
  - no CORS headers;
  - `IPAddressDeny=any`;
  - `POST /v1/gaps` from loopback only;
  - the step request with the config is written with mode 0600.
- **Installed units** match the rendered templates byte for byte.
